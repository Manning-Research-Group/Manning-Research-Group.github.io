Individual Development Plan (IDP)

Each member of the Manning group will work through an individual development plan with Professor Manning once a year. The goal of this process is to help members identify their strengths and areas where there is room for improvement. Using these, as well as preferences for types of work, culture, and environment, together we will make a plan for achieving their career goals.
