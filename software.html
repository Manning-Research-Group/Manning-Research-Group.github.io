Software

Since 2015, software developed by the Manning Group is available at our GitHub repository:

ACTIVE — nuclei tracking

Software for nuclei tracking associated with the following publication:

R. M. Baker, M. E. Brasch, M. L. Manning, J. H. Henderson, “Automated, contour-based tracking and analysis of cell behavior over long timescales in environments of varying complexity and cell density,” J. Roy. Soc. Interface 11(97), 20140386, doi: 10.1098/rsif.2014.0386 (2014).

Available for download from the Henderson website.

Particle tracking in Kupffer’s vesicle (zebrafish)

Software for particle tracking inside Kupffer’s vesicle in the zebrafish, associated with the following book chapter:

Craig Fox, M. Lisa Manning, Jeff Amack. Imaging beads to quantify fluid flow in the ciliated organ of asymmetry in zebrafish. In Methods in Cilia and Flagella (2014).

The download is available here. Please email mmanning@syr.edu with name and affiliation for the password.

New software for simultaneous nuclei and Golgi body tracking will become available here.
